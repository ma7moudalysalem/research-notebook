# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Disagree with the work, not the person. Assume the other person read
less carefully rather than that they argued in bad faith, because most of the
time that is what happened.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
associated with this repository.

## The standard

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

Expected:

- Correcting an error in a summary is a **contribution**, and the most valuable
  one this repository can receive. Say what is wrong and why; a citation helps.
- Disagreement about a paper's claims is normal and welcome. Disagreement about
  someone's competence is not.
- Accept that the summaries here are one person's reading, sometimes wrong, and
  written to be revised.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, including reopening a settled disagreement to have it
  again.

## Enforcement

Report to **ma7moudalysalem@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately
and the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

I will acknowledge within 7 days. Responses range from a private correction to
a permanent block, in proportion to the behaviour and whether it repeats.

I am the sole maintainer, which means I am also the only enforcement mechanism.
If a complaint is about me, GitHub's own
[reporting process](https://docs.github.com/communities) is the escalation, and
using it is legitimate.
