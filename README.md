## How a summary gets written

Three passes, after Keshav's *How to Read a Paper* (ACM SIGCOMM CCR 37(3),
2007). Pass one is five minutes and decides whether to continue; pass two is an
hour with the figures; pass three is re-implementing the argument in your head.

What is mine rather than Keshav's is the machinery around it: a note template
whose required sections scale with how far you have actually read, so a note
cannot claim `read` until its TL;DR, Method, Results, Limitations and Questions
are all genuinely written; and a promotion tool that refuses ten different ways
before anything reaches this repository.

Every summary here also carries the section most reviews skip: **the comparison
the authors did not run.**

## Reproductions rank above summaries

Reading a paper and running one are different claims. A reproduction here comes
with a scorecard — the paper's number, my number, the gap, and a verdict — plus
the part that is usually worth more than the result: what the paper left out of
its own method section.

Upstream code is pinned by commit and cloned outside this repository, never
vendored, and each reproduction records the upstream licence and whether it
permits publishing a derivative at all. Academic repositories very often carry
no licence file, and "no licence" means all rights reserved.

## What is not here

Stated plainly, because absence reads as an oversight otherwise:

- **No publisher PDFs**, in any form, in any commit, ever.
- **No figures or tables** copied from papers. Where a figure appears it was
  either generated by my own reproduction run or redrawn from the text, and it
  carries a `.source.yml` declaring which — with the editable source committed
  alongside it.
- **No abstracts**, verbatim or lightly reworded.
- **No unpublished results, drafts, or thesis material.** Those live in a
  private workspace and cross into this one only through a reviewed,
  one-directional promotion.
- **No dataset content**, not even a sample.

See [`docs/02-what-is-not-here.md`](docs/02-what-is-not-here.md).
